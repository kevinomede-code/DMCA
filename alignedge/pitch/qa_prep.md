# AlignEdge Q&A Preparation — 10 Kill Questions

**Version:** v1 · 2026-07-05
**Purpose:** ready-to-say answers for the 10 most likely brutal Q&A questions after pitch.

Format: question (VC voice) + subtext + model answer (English, 45-60s each) + delivery notes.

## Q1 · SOLO FOUNDER

**VC:** *"You're a master's student pitching solo. In 12 months, who's building this with you? And why haven't you found them yet?"*

**Subtext:** execution risk. Solo founder MSc = statistical pattern of startups dying from bandwidth.

**Answer:**
> *"Fair concern. Here's where I am — the last 8 months I've built the framework end-to-end solo. Five-stage pipeline, drift classifier at 92 percent, canonical benchmark, live simulator. That's technical execution proven.*
>
> *What I need next is not another technical person — I need a commercial co-founder. Someone with tier-one auto sales experience or ML enterprise GTM. I'm actively identifying candidates through this program cohort. Target close: Q4 2026, before I raise seed.*
>
> *If I don't find the right person, I don't hire. Solo build for another 6 months is preferable to wrong co-founder. That's the risk I'm managing consciously."*

**Delivery note:** don't sound defensive. "Fair concern" disarms. End with judgment maturity ("I don't hire wrong person").

## Q2 · CHERRY-PICKING

**VC:** *"The 10× gap on one specific bearing looks impressive. What's the median case? Is Bearing 3_2 your worst-case or your cherry-picked story?"*

**Subtext:** testing whether you stress-tested your own thesis.

**Answer:**
> *"Good question. Bearing 3_2 is the case we show for delivery clarity — same task, same hardware, same SLA, wide coverage spread. The visual anchor.*
>
> *The pattern isn't cherry-picked. All 6 Learning_set bearings on PHM 2012 show the same coverage-driven gap. We replicated on IMS Center — different NASA dataset, 7 days of continuous industrial data — and the ratio there is 11.6. Scale-invariant across accelerated bench and realistic industrial regimes.*
>
> *If you want, I'll send you the full CSV — public data, reproducible. Bearing 3_2 is not the cherry, it's the representative worst-case we use to make the point in 10 seconds."*

**Delivery note:** offer CSV = transparency signal. Reframe from cherry-pick to "representative worst-case".

**VC:** *"OctoAI peaked at $900M and sold to NVIDIA for $165M. That's a downround. Doesn't that tell you cross-vendor optimization isn't a profitable market?"*

**Subtext:** testing industry knowledge + ability to distinguish market segments.

**Answer:**
> *"Fair — the OctoAI story is complicated. They peaked at 900 million during the 2022 AI bubble with single-digit millions in revenue. Hype valuation, not commercial value.*
>
> *More important — they pivoted from cross-vendor optimization to GenAI inference in 2023, abandoned the thesis. NVIDIA acquired them at 165 to absorb the TVM team and close the cross-vendor threat.*

> *But that was cloud inference. Our market is industrial edge audit. Different buyer, different economics, different competitive landscape. ML observability alone is a billion-dollar category — Arize and Fiddler. PdM software is 7 billion. Those markets are profitable. OctoAI's failure teaches us about cloud inference against NVIDIA. It teaches nothing about industrial edge audit."*

## Q9 · TECHNICAL DIFFERENTIATION

**VC (technical partner):** *"92 percent drift accuracy sounds impressive. Arize claims 95, Fiddler 96. What makes yours actually better?"*

**Answer:**
> *"Different metric. Arize and Fiddler measure statistical drift on cloud-side inference — feature distribution changes, prediction distribution changes. Two-class problem.*
>
> *Our classifier is drift signature classification across 7 categories — abrupt, gradual, incremental, variance, distribution, outlier, unclassified. Seven-class problem, held-out, 92 percent accuracy.*
>
> *More importantly, 0 percent false alarm. Harder metric because false alarms destroy operator trust in industrial contexts. Arize doesn't publish false alarm rates.*
>
> *And neither Arize nor Fiddler operates on edge hardware with SLA coverage measurement. Different problem."*

## 5 delivery rules for all Q&A

1. **Start with concession**: "Fair concern" / "Good question" / "Right — [premise]" — disarms the killer
2. **Numbers isolated**: never two numbers connected by "and" — pause between them
3. **Close with definitive statement**: "Not existential" / "Different problem" / "That order is deliberate"
4. **Don't add "does that answer?"**: sounds insecure
5. **If stuck, say**: "Let me answer that in two parts" — buys 3 seconds

---

## 3 rescue phrases when English fails

- *"Let me say that differently."*
- *"Good question. Let me think for a second."*
- *"Can you say that again, a bit slower?"*

Never apologize for accent. Never say "sorry".
