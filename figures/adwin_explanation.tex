\caption{ADWIN adaptive windowing mechanism.
\textit{Left}: the window $W$ grows during
stationary periods and shrinks upon drift
detection. \textit{Centre}: ADWIN2 internal
bucket structure---$O(\log|W|)$ buckets of
geometrically increasing size store (sum,~count)
statistics. \textit{Right}: the statistical
test compares sub-window means $\bar{\mu}_{W_0}$
and $\bar{\mu}_{W_1}$ against the Hoeffding-derived
threshold $\varepsilon_{\text{cut}}$
(Eq.~\ref{eq:adwin-threshold}).}
\label{fig:adwin-mechanism}
